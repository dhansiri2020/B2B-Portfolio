How we would work together.

  1. Define the ideal customer

    We agree on industry, company size, location and the role you want to reach.

  2. Research and build the list

    I collect companies that match, then remove the ones that do not fit.

  3. Find and verify contacts

    Each company gets the right person and an email that has been checked.

  4. Hand over and refine

    You get a clean spreadsheet. After your first outreach we adjust the targeting.
